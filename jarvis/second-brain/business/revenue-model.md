# Revenue Model

## Target

CHF 1,500 net/month.

## Revenue Channels

- AdSense once traffic volume and approval conditions are good enough.
- Affiliate boxes for relevant financial, insurance, productivity, or household offers.
- PDF downloads and saved results.
- Newsletter or lead magnet.
- Service leads from useful calculator traffic.

## Rules

- Do not add fake ad code.
- Do not overload pages with ads.
- Monetization should support the calculator experience.
- Start with the pages that already receive impressions or traffic.
