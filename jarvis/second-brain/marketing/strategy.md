# Marketing Strategy

## Positioning

Calcolich should become a practical Swiss calculator website: fast, useful, simple, and trustworthy.

Primary positioning:

- Online calculators for everyday Swiss questions.
- Focus on salary, work time, VAT, finance, budget, rent, mobility, health, and dates.
- Start with German-speaking Switzerland, then scale to Italian, English, and French.

## Growth Priorities

1. German SEO pages with high practical intent.
2. Useful calculators with original explanations.
3. Long-tail support articles around each calculator cluster.
4. Internal linking between calculator and article pages.
5. Clean technical SEO.
6. Conversion paths for PDF, saved result, newsletter, and service leads.

## What Jarvis Should Avoid

- Publishing many thin pages.
- Generic AI articles.
- Keyword stuffing.
- Adding ads before traffic is meaningful.
- Building features without a traffic or revenue hypothesis.

## 30-Day Focus

- Strengthen the existing German calculator cluster.
- Add new calculators only when they match clear search demand.
- Improve pages with early Search Console impressions.
- Build one support article per proven cluster.
- Start preparing monetization components but keep UX clean.

## 90-Day Focus

- Expand German calculator inventory.
- Translate the strongest calculators to Italian, English, and French.
- Add lead capture where traffic exists.
- Test one monetization channel at a time.
- Build recurring weekly SEO review.
