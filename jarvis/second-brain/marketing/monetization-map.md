# Monetization Map

## AdSense

Use when:

- pages have stable organic traffic;
- content quality is strong;
- page layout can support ads without hurting calculator use.

Rules:

- no fake ad code;
- no aggressive placement above the calculator;
- prioritize readability and trust.

## Affiliate

Best candidate clusters:

- finance;
- loans;
- insurance;
- budget;
- mobility;
- household;
- productivity templates.

Rules:

- affiliate offer must match user intent;
- disclose clearly when needed;
- do not add generic boxes to every page.

## Lead Magnets

Best ideas:

- salary calculation PDF;
- budget checklist;
- VAT example sheet;
- work time planning checklist;
- rent affordability worksheet.

## Services

Possible service lead paths:

- website audit;
- small business calculator tools;
- automation consulting;
- SEO calculator page package.

## Priority Order

1. Traffic first.
2. CTR optimization.
3. Internal linking.
4. Lead magnet.
5. AdSense.
6. Affiliate tests.
7. Service funnel.
