# Business Opportunity Review

Use this before creating a new business stream or cashflow agent.

## Questions

1. What pain or demand exists?
2. Who searches for it or pays for it?
3. Can Giuseppe build or operate it fast?
4. What is the first small asset?
5. What is the first traffic channel?
6. What is the first monetization path?
7. What agent should support it?
8. What would prove this is worth continuing?

## Output

- opportunity summary;
- target audience;
- MVP asset;
- monetization path;
- traffic path;
- agent needed;
- 7-day test;
- stop condition.
